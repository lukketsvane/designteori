                                                      fast permeating everyday life for people around the world (Jandric and Hayes, 2022). The
                                          pace of digital technology development is fast outstripping the pace in which people
                                           aﬀected can understand and shape its future. There is also an overall concern with the
                                              future on the rise. Catastrophes such as climate change, wars, a global pandemic, political
                                               polarization underline the pressing need to imagine alternative futures. Developing critical
                                          awareness and curiosity can be useful in pushing back against the seeming inevitability
                                        (Zenz and Powles, 2024) of emerging technologies, driven by visions of calculable futures
                                          (Williamson and Komljenovic, 2023). There is a need to cultivate our capacity to imagine
                                                 alternative futures to tip the scale toward the future outcomes that we prefer. We suggest
                                                that design ﬁction provides a useful avenue for doing exactly this.
