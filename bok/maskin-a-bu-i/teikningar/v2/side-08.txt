Make one finished square 1:1 page, edge-to-edge pure white paper, black pen only. An original, sober visual argument about Bergen-Belsen, not a historical scene. Let the reader follow one mundane shape: a rectangle that first sorts, then measures, then leaves its mark on the hand that drew it. Use economical, slightly unsteady freehand contours, small variations in nib pressure, open white interiors and a great deal of untouched paper. Small, legible, informally hand-lettered uppercase annotations beside the things they name. No typeset lettering. Invent the figures and composition; do not reproduce figures, handwriting or page layouts from the references.

Arrange three unframed studies at unequal heights:

• Upper left, spreading a little past the middle: a small anonymous barracks plan seen straight down. Two short rows of narrow, empty rectangular footprints. At the right end of the plan two footprints are ALSO the bottom boxes of an organisational chart: their wall strokes continue upwards to meet at a fork beneath one empty box. Small arrowheads point DOWN the fork. Make this a single ambiguous drawing, not a map next to a flowchart. Beside the footprints, in small writing with a short leader, write “BERGEN-BELSEN”. By the two different ends of the fork write “ARBEIDSFØRE” and “FOR SJUKE TIL Å BRUKAST” respectively. Keep writing outside the boxes; the longer label may wrap after TIL.

• Further right and a little lower, a quieter study of allotted air. One plan rectangle lifts into a small empty wireframe cube. Repeat it immediately to its right as a much flatter box, then so flat that only a blank paper slip with an empty name line remains. Three spare outlines, a visible passage from volume to nothing. Above the cube, write “KUBIKKMETER LUFT PER KROPP” on two short lines, with a fine leader into its empty interior. Just below the last slip write “TØMT FOR NAMN”. No numerical measurements.

• The main drawing crosses the lower half on a gentle diagonal. At left, two loose sheets of tracing paper with one corner lifted, transparency conveyed only by visible overlapping outlines, never by tone. A simple right-angled partition is traced on each. The two tracings register except for one wall shifted a hair’s breadth sideways. A tiny crosswise dimension bracket picks out the narrow white interval. Beside that bracket, write “TO MILLIMETER”. Follow the shifted ink line past the lifted paper edge: on the right it becomes the crease of a single open drawing hand. Palm facing us, five relaxed fingers, seen obliquely, wrist ending in two simple unfinished strokes. Draw the hand with very few fluid outlines. Within its palm the crease branches into the same tiny arrangement of empty boxes and downward arrows seen above: the sheet lifts away, the diagram stays in the skin. No wound, no stain. Leave white space beneath the sheets for a small handwritten note at lower left, with a loose leader ending at that palm crease. The note reads exactly, in three lines:
“EIT ORGANISASJONSKART
KAN IKKJE VASKE HENDENE
PÅ DEN SOM HAR TEIKNA DET.”

The quoted annotations are ALL the visible text. Render each once, without quotation marks, preserving Ø and Å exactly. No title, page number, signature, invented writing or decorative border. No other figures, faces, scenes of suffering, weapons, emblems or war symbols. No pencil motif from the references. No shading, hatching, gray, color, shadow, paper grain or photographic effects. Let the white space and the transformations of the line carry the meaning.
