Make one square (1:1) page of original speculative freehand drawing. Flat white paper edge to edge, black pen only. The visual thought that holds the page together: a whole ceiling light fits inside a grain of stone; later, a whole dark hall fits between two flashes. Make the viewer discover this rhyme through the shapes. Not a narrative scene, poster, or textbook infographic. Leave generous, irregular stretches of untouched paper between the drawings. Use a fine, slightly wavering ink contour, an occasional thicker stroke where the nib lingers, sparse little leaders and open arrowheads. Small, loose, legible handwritten CAPITALS, with slightly uneven spacing and baseline. No borrowing of specific figures or page arrangements from the references.

Upper-left and upper-middle: high on the paper draw a long, slender fluorescent tube, almost horizontal but rising a little, with a distinctive collar at either end. Below and to its right, draw a small angular chip of floor stone with one polished, lentil-shaped inclusion. Ink the inclusion flat black, reserving within it a tiny WHITE image of the COMPLETE long tube, both end collars visible: a reflection, not a crack. To the left of the stone, two tiny viewing points stand a fingertip-width apart over a short sideways measuring bracket. A fine broken sightline from the first just misses the grain; a line from the shifted point arrives precisely at the miniature lamp. Handwrite “EIN TOMME TIL SIDA” just below the bracket. Place “I ÉI LINSE AV STEIN” to the right of the chip with a short leader ending on the inclusion.

Middle-right, the largest gesture: two lightly outlined fingertips pinch a pencil near its blunt end. The pencil points upward. It is drawn fully only at the left extreme of its movement, with wood shoulder and graphite point; pivoting from the same pinch, its other positions open to the right as a fan of seven separate black strokes with free pointed ends. White air between strokes, no webbing, no motion blur, no loops of pencil lead. Put a little back-and-forth curved arrow beyond the tips, with “ATT OG FRAM” beside it. In the open space to the left of this fan, beneath the lamp, make a small timing study: one freely drawn alternating wave of three complete cycles, with a separate short upright blink mark aligned to each crest AND each trough, two blinks per cycle. No axes or numbers. The local handwritten note below this study is “HUNDRE BLINK AV FEMTI HEILE SVINGINGAR I SEKUNDET.”, wrapped after “AV”.

Lower third: repeat the blink marks as a short, horizontal sequence. At the right they draw closer and fuse into ONE uninterrupted slender line. Just above this continuous end place the note “MEDVITET SER SAMANHENGANDE DAGSLYS.” on two lines, a small pointer touching the line. At the left of the sequence, select the narrow WHITE GAP between two distinct marks. From the two edges of that same gap draw two fine diverging magnification lines down to a tiny elongated, flat-black corridor in plan. Two white door thresholds and thin white wall returns cut into its sides: an entire building interior contained in an instant, not a shaded perspective view. Its shape quietly recalls the dark stone inclusion above. Handwrite “HEILE HALLEN I MØRKRE.” beside this miniature plan, pointing into its black interior.

Only the six quoted Nynorsk inscriptions may appear, each once, exactly spelled, including É and Ø. Do not print quotation marks. No title, running head, page number, signature, English, incidental glyphs or other text. No panel borders, typeset lettering, grayscale, hatching, gray wash, shadows, paper texture or color. Keep all ink well inside the square.
