## Første kjeldebaserte spor (ikkje validerte fagkodar)

### Understanding of a product/service system design (2014)

**Påstandar:** P03, P10. **PDF-sider:** 11-12. **[Original DOI](https://doi.org/10.1186/2210-4690-4-1) · [Arkivert PDF](https://github.com/lukketsvane/designteori/blob/main/fulltekst/pdf/W2126722450.pdf).**

The article rejects the null of independence between strategy/PSS-dimension categories in its protocol-analysis utterance tables using chi-square tests, reporting p < 0.01. It reports 1450 coded utterances from one PSS design episode.

**Avgrensing:** Null of statistical independence is not the same as a prior directional design theory prediction; one episode does not establish generality, causal identification, or independent replication; potential dependence among utterances requires methodological evaluation.

**Vidare kontroll:** Check table construction, independence assumptions and whether tested categories/analyses were prespecified; trace later independent reuse.

### Uncertainty Driven Action (UDA) model (2017)

**Påstandar:** P01, P03. **PDF-sider:** 3-4;30-33. **[Original DOI](https://doi.org/10.1017/dsj.2017.28) · [Arkivert PDF](https://github.com/lukketsvane/designteori/blob/main/fulltekst/pdf/W2774875954.pdf).**

Authors synthesize prior design activity literature and identify three propositions described as concrete claims intended for future empirical testing; discussion starts at PDF page 31.

**Avgrensing:** Stating a proposition for future testing is not actual testing, validation, replication, a multi-group sustained program, or empirical support for that proposition.

**Vidare kontroll:** Transcribe each exact proposition with page and see whether later independent studies operationalize, test, revise or reject it.

### Design Thinking as a Master Idea (2022)

**Påstandar:** P05, P06. **PDF-sider:** 1-2;11-13. **[Original DOI](https://doi.org/10.7557/13.6718) · [Arkivert PDF](https://github.com/lukketsvane/designteori/blob/main/fulltekst/pdf/W4311715142.pdf).**

Authors explicitly interpret Design Thinking as a master idea and rationalized myth; PDF pages 11-13 discuss legitimacy sustained by belief in effectiveness, generalized applicability and organizational networks rather than documented effects.

**Avgrensing:** An argument about legitimation is not by itself a causal, representative demonstration that all design commissions were predetermined or that all design research is invalid.

**Vidare kontroll:** Check actual empirical sources behind the claims; distinguish design management discourse from disciplinary evidence tests.

### Supporting and understanding students' collaborative reflection-in-action during design-based learning (2023)

**Påstandar:** P03, P05, P10. **PDF-sider:** 1;9;13;34. **[Original DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10798-023-09814-0) · [Arkivert PDF](https://github.com/lukketsvane/designteori/blob/main/fulltekst/pdf/W4321436228.pdf).**

Reports a field trial of the ReflectionSchema tool with 13 groups of students. Methods report a class of 28 students, 204 videos captured and 134 retained for analysis; final discussion flags single-school context and limited transferability.

**Avgrensing:** One class and a qualitative/quantitative pilot do not demonstrate independent validation or causal effect; interpretation depends on exact design, comparisons and outcome definitions.

**Vidare kontroll:** Read instrument and analytic methods, check exclusions/denominators and any baseline or control group before counting H3 or C2.

### Designing learning technology collaboratively: Analysis of a chatbot co-design (2022)

**Påstandar:** P05, P09. **PDF-sider:** 5;7-9;14. **[Original DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10639-022-11162-w) · [Arkivert PDF](https://github.com/lukketsvane/designteori/blob/main/fulltekst/pdf/W4283392104.pdf).**

Describes action research with questionnaires/workshops and discusses concrete conflicts about student privacy, monitoring and who controls data in the chatbot design process.

**Avgrensing:** Discourse about an ethical dilemma is not evidence of effective institutional sanctions, implemented protections, or avoided harm.

**Vidare kontroll:** Check whether recorded privacy concerns produced a documented revision to implementation and what evidence supports this.

## Ikkje tolka som resultat

Dei fem spora er valde ut frå tilgjengelege PDF-ar og relevant innhald.
Dei er ikkje eit tilfeldig utval. Sidetilvising og lesing med eit AI-verktøy
er ikkje ein uavhengig menneskeleg kodar, og oppfyller ikkje E2/E3/E4.
Ingen konklusjonar om representativ frekvens, institusjonell korrupsjon
eller heile forskingsfeltet er tekne på dette grunnlaget.

Det eldre 600-utvalet står framleis på 0 dobbelkoda fulltekstar.
Nasjonale register, fleire sjangrar, kontrollfag og reelle kodarar må til
før ei etterprøvbar vitskapsteoretisk generalisering.
